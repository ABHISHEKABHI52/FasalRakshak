# 07 · Database Design

PostgreSQL 16 + PostGIS + pgvector. Normalized (3NF for transactional entities). Conventions: UUID primary keys (time-ordered, e.g., UUIDv7) [PROPOSED DESIGN]; `timestamptz` UTC everywhere; snake_case; enums as CHECK constraints (portable) or native enums; soft-activation via `is_active` (no destructive deletes on shared data). Status: [PROPOSED DESIGN] — DDL generated by Alembic migrations; this document is the canonical schema spec.

## 1. Identity & Access

### users
Purpose: all human actors. Columns: `id UUID PK` · `phone VARCHAR(20) UNIQUE` · `email VARCHAR(255) UNIQUE NULL` · `password_hash TEXT` (Argon2id) · `full_name VARCHAR(120)` · `preferred_language VARCHAR(8) DEFAULT 'hi'` · `district VARCHAR(80)` · `state VARCHAR(80)` · `consent_ml_use BOOLEAN DEFAULT FALSE` · `is_active BOOLEAN DEFAULT TRUE` · `created_at TIMESTAMPTZ` · `last_login_at TIMESTAMPTZ NULL`. Indexes: phone, email. Constraints: at least one of phone/email (CHECK).

### roles & user_roles
`roles`: `id SMALLINT PK` · `name VARCHAR(32) UNIQUE` (farmer | extension_worker | expert | district_officer | admin) · `description TEXT`. `user_roles`: `user_id FK→users` · `role_id FK→roles` · PK(user_id, role_id). **M:N** — a user (e.g., extension worker) may hold farmer+worker roles.

### refresh_tokens
`id UUID PK` · `user_id FK` · `token_hash TEXT UNIQUE` · `expires_at TIMESTAMPTZ` · `revoked_at TIMESTAMPTZ NULL` · `created_at`. Index: (user_id, expires_at).

## 2. Farm, Field & Crop Structure

### farms
`id UUID PK` · `owner_id FK→users` · `name VARCHAR(120)` · `address TEXT` · `district VARCHAR(80)` · `state VARCHAR(80)` · `is_active BOOLEAN` · `created_at`. Index: (owner_id). One farmer → many farms.

### fields
`id UUID PK` · `farm_id FK→farms` · `name VARCHAR(120)` · `area_hectares NUMERIC(6,2) NULL` · `location geography(Point,4326)` (PostGIS; GIST index) · `soil_type VARCHAR(40) NULL` · `district_code VARCHAR(10) NULL` · `is_active` · `created_at`. Index: GIST(location), (farm_id). **Geometry usage:** proximity queries (`ST_DWithin`), distance sorting, heatmap binning, district rollups.

### crops
Catalogue: `id UUID PK` · `code VARCHAR(40) UNIQUE` (tomato|potato|cotton|…) · `name_en VARCHAR(80)` · `name_hi VARCHAR(80)` · `scientific_name VARCHAR(120) NULL` · `stage_model JSONB` (stage thresholds by GDD/days — prototype) · `is_supported BOOLEAN` (MVP: tomato, potato, cotton).

### crop_cycles
`id UUID PK` · `field_id FK→fields` · `crop_id FK→crops` · `variety VARCHAR(80) NULL` · `sowing_date DATE` · `expected_harvest_date DATE NULL` · `current_stage VARCHAR(32)` (derived: seedling/vegetative/flowering/fruiting/maturity) · `stage_updated_at` · `status VARCHAR(16)` (active/completed/abandoned). Index: (field_id, sowing_date). One field → many cycles (seasons).

## 3. Scanning & AI Artifacts

### crop_scans
`id UUID PK` · `client_scan_uuid UUID UNIQUE` (idempotency) · `field_id FK` · `crop_cycle_id FK` · `user_id FK` · `plant_part VARCHAR(24)` (leaf/stem/fruit/whole_plant/trap) · `captured_at TIMESTAMPTZ` · `image_id FK→images NULL` · `status VARCHAR(24)` (pending_image|analyzing|answered|completed|failed) · `created_at`. Indexes: (field_id, created_at DESC), status. One field → many scans.

### images
`id UUID PK` · `storage_path TEXT` (object key) · `mime_type VARCHAR(40)` · `size_bytes INT` · `width INT` · `height INT` · `checksum_sha256 CHAR(64) UNIQUE` (dedupe) · `created_at`. Constraint: mime in (image/jpeg, image/png, image/webp).

### image_quality_results
`id UUID PK` · `scan_id FK→crop_scans UNIQUE` (1:1) · `image_id FK` · `quality_score SMALLINT CHECK 0–100` · `category VARCHAR(12)` (good|acceptable|poor|unusable) · `reasons JSONB` · `leaf_coverage REAL NULL` · `usable BOOLEAN` · `model_ref VARCHAR(40) NULL` · `created_at`.

### model_registry
`id UUID PK` · `name VARCHAR(60)` · `version VARCHAR(20)` · `UNIQUE(name, version)` · `artifact_uri TEXT` · `artifact_sha256 CHAR(64)` · `metrics JSONB` · `thresholds JSONB` (confidence/OOD per class) · `status VARCHAR(12)` (shadow|active|retired) · `activated_at` · `created_at`.

### ai_predictions
`id UUID PK` · `scan_id FK` · `model_id FK→model_registry` · `prediction_type VARCHAR(12)` (crop_id|disease|pest) · `label_code VARCHAR(60) NULL` · `label_confidence REAL` · `topk JSONB` · `ood_score REAL NULL` · `is_sufficient BOOLEAN` · `explainability_ref TEXT NULL` · `latency_ms INT` · `created_at`. Index: (scan_id), (model_id, created_at).

### pest_detections
`id UUID PK` · `ai_prediction_id FK→ai_predictions` · `pest_code VARCHAR(60)` · `confidence REAL` · `bbox JSONB` (xyxy normalized) · `count_estimate INT NULL`.

## 4. Inquiry, Diagnosis, Risk & Advisory

### question_bank
`id UUID PK` · `code VARCHAR(60) UNIQUE` · `text_en TEXT` · `text_hi TEXT` · `answer_type VARCHAR(16)` (choice|boolean|number|text) · `options JSONB` (for choice) · `tags JSONB` (crop, symptom_family, context_need, hypothesis_relevance) · `evidence_updates JSONB` (per-answer likelihood updates — rule-based, reviewable) · `is_active`. Index: GIN(tags).

### question_answers
`id UUID PK` · `scan_id FK` · `question_id FK→question_bank` · `answer_value JSONB` · `answered_at TIMESTAMPTZ`. Index: (scan_id). **1 scan → N answers** (per round).

### diagnoses
`id UUID PK` · `scan_id FK→crop_scans UNIQUE` (1:1 with scan; row updated as state evolves) · `final_status VARCHAR(24)` (diagnosed|insufficient_evidence|referred_expert|expert_confirmed|expert_corrected|rejected) · `primary_label_code VARCHAR(60) NULL` · `fused_confidence REAL NULL` · `differential JSONB` (top-k with fused probabilities) · `evidence_trace JSONB` (which inputs moved which hypothesis) · `final_label_code VARCHAR(60) NULL` (post-validation) · `created_at` · `updated_at`.

### risk_predictions
`id UUID PK` · `field_id FK→fields` · `scan_id FK NULL` (field-level risks have NULL) · `risk_type VARCHAR(8)` (cur|3d|7d|14d) · `score SMALLINT CHECK 0–100` · `category VARCHAR(10)` (low|medium|high|critical) · `factors JSONB` (named drivers + contributions) · `weather_completeness JSONB` · `model_ref VARCHAR(60)` (ruleset version or XGBoost version) · `created_at`. Index: (field_id, risk_type, created_at DESC).

### recommendations
`id UUID PK` · `diagnosis_id FK→diagnoses` · `locale VARCHAR(8)` · `steps JSONB` (ordered: text, icon_key) · `sources JSONB` (title, publisher, year, url, chunk_ref) · `safety_note TEXT` · `generated_by VARCHAR(16)` (template|llm_grounded) · `generator_version VARCHAR(20)` · `created_at`. **One diagnosis → N localized recommendation rows.**

## 5. Knowledge (RAG)

### knowledge_documents
`id UUID PK` · `title TEXT` · `publisher VARCHAR(120)` (ICAR | state agri university | DPPQS/extension | …) · `source_url TEXT NULL` · `publication_year SMALLINT NULL` · `language VARCHAR(8)` · `doc_type VARCHAR(24)` (advisory|ipm_guide|package_of_practices|circular) · `file_path TEXT` · `checksum_sha256 UNIQUE` · `ingestion_status VARCHAR(12)` (pending|processed|failed) · `created_at`.

### knowledge_chunks
`id UUID PK` · `document_id FK→knowledge_documents` · `chunk_index INT` · `content TEXT` · `embedding vector(384)` (dimension per chosen embedding model; pgvector) · `tags JSONB` (crop, pest/disease codes, stage, region) · `token_count SMALLINT`. Index: HNSW on embedding; GIN(tags); (document_id, chunk_index) unique.

## 6. Weather

### weather_observations
`id UUID PK` · `field_id FK→fields` · `observed_at TIMESTAMPTZ` · `is_forecast BOOLEAN` · `temperature_c REAL NULL` · `humidity_pct REAL NULL CHECK 0–100` · `rainfall_mm REAL NULL CHECK ≥0` · `wind_kmph REAL NULL` · `payload JSONB` (raw provider response slice) · `provider VARCHAR(30)` · `data_age_hours REAL` · `completeness JSONB` (which variables present). Index: (field_id, observed_at DESC). Retention: raw 90 days, aggregates kept.

## 7. Surveillance & Workflow

### field_observations
`id UUID PK` · `field_id FK` · `observer_id FK→users` · `observation_type VARCHAR(20)` (farmer_report|expert_visit|officer_inspection) · `disease_code VARCHAR(60) NULL` · `pest_code VARCHAR(60) NULL` · `severity VARCHAR(10) NULL` · `notes TEXT` · `observed_at TIMESTAMPTZ` · `created_at`. Index: (field_id, observed_at DESC).

### disease_reports
`id UUID PK` · `field_id FK` · `reported_by FK→users` · `source VARCHAR(12)` (farmer|ai|expert|officer) · `disease_code VARCHAR(60)` · `status VARCHAR(16)` (reported|ai_predicted|expert_verified|rejected) · `report_date DATE` · `verified_by FK→users NULL` · `confidence REAL NULL` (for AI-sourced) · `created_at`. Index: (district via field join), (disease_code, status), (report_date). **Status semantics enforce the product-wide distinction: reported ≠ ai_predicted ≠ expert_verified.**

### pest_reports
Same shape as `disease_reports` with `pest_code`; shared constraint pattern.

### gis_hotspots
`id UUID PK` · `district VARCHAR(80)` · `centroid geography(Point,4326)` (GIST) · `cluster_size INT` · `threat_type VARCHAR(8)` (disease|pest) · `threat_code VARCHAR(60)` · `status VARCHAR(12)` (candidate|confirmed|dismissed) · `trend VARCHAR(10)` (rising|stable|declining) · `first_seen_at` · `last_updated_at` · `parameters JSONB` (eps_m, min_reports, algorithm version). 

### hotspot_members
`hotspot_id FK` · `report_type VARCHAR(12)` (disease_report|pest_report) · `report_id UUID` · PK(hotspot_id, report_type, report_id). **M:N between reports and hotspots** (a report joins one current hotspot; history preserved via period snapshots **[ADVANCED]**).

### expert_reviews
`id UUID PK` · `diagnosis_id FK→diagnoses` · `expert_id FK→users` · `verdict VARCHAR(10)` (confirmed|corrected|rejected) · `original_prediction JSONB` (frozen copy incl. model version + confidence) · `corrected_label_code VARCHAR(60) NULL` · `remarks TEXT` · `reviewed_at TIMESTAMPTZ`. Index: (expert_id, reviewed_at), (diagnosis_id). **Dataset-ready:** original vs corrected + context snapshot → training export.

### notifications
`id UUID PK` · `user_id FK` · `type VARCHAR(30)` (risk_alert|verdict_ready|hotspot_new|system) · `payload JSONB` · `read_at TIMESTAMPTZ NULL` · `created_at`. Index: (user_id, created_at DESC), partial WHERE read_at IS NULL.

### alerts
`id UUID PK` · `dedupe_key TEXT UNIQUE` (e.g., `field:{id}:threat:{code}:{window}`) · `severity VARCHAR(8)` (LOW|MEDIUM|HIGH|CRITICAL) · `audience VARCHAR(10)` (farmer|officer|expert) · `field_id FK NULL` · `district VARCHAR(80) NULL` · `threat_code VARCHAR(60) NULL` · `message_key TEXT` (i18n key) · `status VARCHAR(10)` (open|acknowledged|resolved|expired) · `created_at` · `expires_at`.

### feedback
`id UUID PK` · `user_id FK` · `scan_id FK NULL` · `diagnosis_id FK NULL` · `rating VARCHAR(10)` (useful|not_useful) · `is_expert_feedback BOOLEAN` · `dataset_tag VARCHAR(40) NULL` (export manifest tag) · `comment TEXT NULL` · `created_at`.

### audit_logs
`id BIGSERIAL PK` (append-only; no updates/deletes via app role) · `user_id FK NULL` · `action VARCHAR(40)` · `resource_type VARCHAR(30)` · `resource_id UUID NULL` · `ip_hash CHAR(64)` · `before JSONB NULL` · `after JSONB NULL` · `created_at`. Index: (resource_type, resource_id), (user_id, created_at).

## 8. ER Relationships Summary

- **1:1** — scan↔image (via crop_scans.image_id), scan↔quality result, scan↔diagnosis.
- **1:N** — user→farms, farm→fields, field→cycles, field→scans, scan→predictions, prediction→pest detections, diagnosis→recommendations, document→chunks, field→weather/risk/observations/reports, user→notifications/feedback.
- **M:N** — users↔roles (user_roles), reports↔hotspots (hotspot_members), crops↔regions applicability `crop_region_applicability` **[P2]**.

## 9. PostGIS Usage Patterns

| Pattern | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Field registration | `ST_MakePoint(lon,lat)` cast to `geography(Point,4326)` |
| Nearby fields | `ST_DWithin(location, :pt, :radius_m)` using GIST index |
| Heatmap | hex/grid binning via `ST_SnapToGrid` + COUNT aggregation, materialized per district |
| Hotspot clustering | DBSCAN over reported/verified points (window-function/PL-pgSQL implementation, eps in meters on geography) |
| District rollups | points-in-polygon counts against `regions` boundary table **[P2]** |

## 10. Data Retention & Integrity

Backups: nightly `pg_dump` + WAL archiving **[P2]**; scans/images immutable (append-only artifacts); personal-data deletion flow anonymizes user linkage (docs/31); migration discipline: Alembic, backward-compatible, reviewed.
